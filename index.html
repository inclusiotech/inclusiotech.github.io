Certified WCAG 2.2 AAA Digital Accessibility Engineering

Resilience Industries Limited (RIL) delivers premier web accessibility testing, manual assistive technology audits, and VPAT / ACR authoring complying with W3C WCAG 2.2 (A, AA, AAA) and Section 508 standards.

Our testing pipelines are led by a certified 100% blind accessibility specialist, guaranteeing authentic validation across native screen readers (TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS) alongside automated code auditing.

Core Competencies & Services

  • Manual Screen Reader Auditing: Native TalkBack & NVDA exploratory and structural analysis.
  • VPAT / ACR Production: Formal ITI standard compliance reporting for procurement.
  • Remediation Engineering: Direct patch-level solutions for front-end development teams.
  • Assistive Tech Consulting: Accessible workflow architecture for organizations.
